# External EnCodec medical-audio benchmark

The pretrained EnCodec point is 1.5 kbps, three times the nominal rate of the closest custom cells. This is an external quality anchor, **not a matched-rate ranking**.

## Result

EnCodec adds 17.62 overall WER points [15.74, 19.50], versus 95.03 for VQ-VAE 500 and 105.68 for FSQ 500. Absolute reconstructed WER is 2.63x higher for VQ-VAE and 2.85x higher for FSQ. Mechanically, EnCodec realizes 64.2% entropy utilization, compared with 40.1% for VQ-VAE and 13.4% for FSQ. These ratios are descriptive because EnCodec receives 3x the nominal bitrate.
